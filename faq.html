ABOUT MARVIS

That is a secret we will never tell. Especially because we don’t know. What we do know is that the Ludovico Martelli company acquired Marvis in 1997 and turned it into the iconic and unconventional toothpaste it is today.

It’s our Marquis. Did he invent toothpaste? Not sure about it. Did he found Marvis? Hard to tell. Was he from Florence? We don’t know. But the real question is: why do you care so much?

It’s all written in the Marquis’ diary. Wherever it’s hidden.
Marvis toothpastes contain a blend of cleaning powders carefully selected so as to ensure excellent cleaning without the risk of damaging enamel and dentin of the tooth.
Moreover, they’re made with an old-style, extremely rich and concentrated paste. This choice not only makes for a compact and pleasant texture, but also allows for no preservatives to be used in the products. Also, if you happen to find the diary, please let us know.

We’re fully committed to running out of toothpaste flavors to produce.
