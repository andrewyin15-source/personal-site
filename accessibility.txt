<ul>

<li>Review of a new sample of pages by July 2027;</li>
<li>Adoption of guidelines and best practices for the creation of new content in compliance with accessibility requirements.</li>
</ul>
<h3 class="mt-4 mb-2">Verification Methodology</h3>
<p>Manual evaluation of a representative sample of pages reflecting the various methods of publishing information on the website, namely:</p>
<ul>

<li>Product detail page;</li>
<li>About us;</li>
<li>Contact;</li>
<li>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ).</li>

<li>NVDA Screen Reader in combination with Firefox Desktop;</li>
<li>JAWS Screen Reader in combination with Google Chrome Desktop;</li>
<li>TALKBACK Screen Reader in combination with Google Chrome Android.</li>
</ul>
<p>Where semi-automated evaluation tools were required, the following were used:</p>
<ul>
<li>Color-pair Contrast Testing;</li>
<li>Web Developer Toolbar;</li>
<li>Visual ARIA;</li>
<li>W3C HTML Validator;</li>
<li>Google Lighthouse;</li>
<li>Mauve++.</li>
</ul>

<p>The evaluation was carried out using a sample-based verification methodology. <br />

<ul>
<li>European Standard EN 301 549 – Accessibility requirements for ICT products and services (UNI EN 301549);</li>
<li>Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2.</li>
</ul>
